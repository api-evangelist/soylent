In a nation known for abundance and prosperity, it is disheartening to acknowledge that not all individuals have equal access to nutritious food. Despite efforts to combat hunger and improve food security, a significant nutrition gap persists among people living in poverty in the United States. In this article, we will explore the challenges surrounding food inequality, the consequences of limited access to nutritious options, and the role that affordable solutions like Soylent can play in bridging the nutrition divide.
