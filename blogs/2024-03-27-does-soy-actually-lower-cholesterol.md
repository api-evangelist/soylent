Summarized from The Journal of the American Heart Association Full Text TLDR Soy protein emerges as a viable component of cholesterol-lowering diets, backed by robust evidence from cumulative a meta-analysis. Despite lingering doubts, soy's role in reducing LDL-C remains significant and merits recognition in dietary guidelines and public health initiatives. As the global emphasis on plant-based protein sources grows, soy stands out as a versatile and effective option for promoting heart health and overall well-being.
